void BipartitioningMapGenerator.CreateDecorationPositions ( MapData mapData)
inline

Places decorations randomly on some floor positions in the map data. This method iterates through the floor positions of each room and places decorations with a 2% chance. Decorations can be either destructible or indestructible, with a 50/50 chance.

void BipartitioningMapGenerator.CreateDoorPositions ( MapData mapData)
inline

Determines the positions of doors by finding the corridors. This method iterates through the floor positions of each room and places doors at positions where the room borders intersect with corridors. Doors are placed exactly in the middle of the corridor if the gap is less than the corridor width wide.

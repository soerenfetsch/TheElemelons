void SaveTilemapData ()
 Saves the current tilemap data by storing the positions and tiles into the tilemapData object. This method iterates through all positions within the tilemap's cell bounds, checks for non-null tiles, and adds their positions and tile references to the tilemapData lists. The changes are then made persistent by marking the tilemapData asset as dirty and saving the assets.
